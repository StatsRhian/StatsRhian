- 🧮 I'm a **data scientist** at [The Strategy Unit](https://www.strategyunitwm.nhs.uk/), improving the **robustness and transparency** of analytic work within the NHS. I build **open-source Reproducible Analytical Pipelines** and give public health analysts the **tools, skills, and confidence** to develop their own reproducible analyses.

- 🗣️ I love **talking statistics**. As a [Statistical Ambassador](https://rss.org.uk/membership/promoting-statistics/rss-statistical-ambassadors/) for the [Royal Statistical Society](https://rss.org.uk), I engage with **the media, schools and the wider public** to improve **statistical literacy**. I'm also a [regular speaker](https://rhian.rbind.io/talks) at **meetups and conferences**.

- 👩‍💻 I'm an **#RStats educator** and [Posit certified trainer](https://education.rstudio.com/trainers/#people). I **write and deliver training courses** and **run workshops** at conferences and community events internationally.

- 💜 I **value community**, and am an active supporter of a number of organisations including [R-Ladies+](https://rladies.org/) and the [NHSR Community](https://nhsrcommunity.com/). I'm part of the global leadership team for [rainbowR](https://rainbowr.org/).
